Adjusting the speed of the gripper

The speed of the gripper is adjusted via the Throttle-check non-return valveFunctionally integrated pneumatic component that combines a throttle-check valve with a parallel non-return valve. Throttle-check non-return valves are used to regulate airflow in a given direction..
PREREQUISITE
Warning:
  • Automatic start-up
  • Parts can move unexpectedly when working on an operational machine, causing serious and potentially deadly injuries.
  • If possible, disconnect or switch off parts of the machine that do not need to be operational.
  • Secure the machine against unintended start-up.
  • The gantry robot is fully assembled.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is fully installed.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is switched on via valve 0Z2.
  • The electric control circuit is fully installed.
  • The electric control circuit is switched on.
  • Any controllers that can cause automatic start-up are switched off or disconnected.
  • The gripper is closed.
The throttle-check non-return valves throttle the exhaust air to prevent rapid pressure drops and slip-stick effects. 2V2 controls closing speed. 2V3 controls opening speed.
  1. Turn the adjusting screws of 2V2 and 2V3 clockwise until the valves are completely closed.
  2. Turn both adjusting screws counter-clockwise by 10 ° to open the valves again a bit.
  3. Check the opening speed:
    1. Press and hold button S2 to open the gripper.
    2. Observe the opening speed.
  4. Check the closing speed:
    1. Release button S2 to close the gripper.
    2. Observe the closing speed.
  5. Adjust the opening speed of the gripper by turning the adjusting screw of 2V3.
    • Clockwise: decrease speed.
    • Counter-clockwise: increase speed.
  6. Adjust the closing speed of the gripper by turning the adjusting screw of 2V2.
    • Clockwise: decrease speed.
    • Counter-clockwise: increase speed.
  7. Manually activate and release valve 2V1 to check whether the speed is appropriate.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: If the speed is not appropriate, continue adjusting the speed.
AFTER COMPLETING THE TASK
  • Adjust the end-position cushioning of the gripper.
