Connecting the gripper

The pneumatic gripper must be connected to the Throttle-check non-return valveFunctionally integrated pneumatic component that combines a throttle-check valve with a parallel non-return valve. Throttle-check non-return valves are used to regulate airflow in a given direction..
PREREQUISITE
  • The gantry robot is assembled.
  • The house containing the is mounted in a proper location.
  • The compressed air supply is switched off.
  • Electrical power is switched off.
Tools needed:
  • Wire cutter
Supply needed:
  • 3-mm tubing
  • Zip ties
  1. Important: Always make straight cuts to avoid improper and leaky connections.
    Cut two tubes into two pieces of appropriate lengths.
  2. Connect the rear connection of the cylinder to connection P3 of the housing.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: P3 connects to connection 2 of valve 2V2.
  3. Connect the rear connection of the cylinder to connection P4 of the housing.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: P4 connects to connection 2 of valve 2V3.
