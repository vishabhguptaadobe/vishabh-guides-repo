Assembling the robot

The robot is assembled as part of a larger machine.
PREREQUISITE
Prepare the following tools:
  • Hex key size 2.5
  • Hex key size 4
  • Metric wrench size 17
  • Spirit level
Attention: All personnel shall wear safety shoes. <-
  1. Mount the stepper motor:Mount the stepper motor:
  2. Mount the stepper motor on the mounting bracket using 4 M3 screws.
  3. Mount the mounting bracket on the plain linear bearing using 4 M5 screws.
  4. Connect the stepper motor and the leadscrew via the coupling.
  5. If necessary, re-adjust the stepper motor, coupling, and mounting bracket to reduce mechanical tensions.
  6. Mount the plain linear bearingMount the plain linear bearing
  7. Place the linear bearing on the columns.
  8. Put spacers between the bearing and the column to compensate for small level differences.
  9. Connect the linear bearing to the columns with 4 M10 screws per column.
  10. Important: Position the ensemble as precisely as possible. You can make millimeter adjustments later.
    Move the bearing with the columns to the target location.
  11. Fix the columns to the ground with 4 M10 screws per column.
  12. Assemble the gripper:Assemble the gripper:
  13. Mount the jaws of the gripper using 2 M3 screws per jaw.
  14. Mount the gripper on the RC servo using 4 M5 screws.
  15. Mount the RC servo on the servo mounting plate using 4 M5 screws.
  16. Connect the piston rod cylinder:Connect the piston rod cylinder:
  17. Mount the cylinder on the cylinder mounting plate using 4 M5 screws.
  18. Mount the cylinder mounting plate on the carriage of the plain linear bearing.
  19. Screw the piston rod into the servo mounting plate.
  20. Secure the piston rod with the piston rod nut.
  21. If necessary, adjust the position of the cylinder on the mounting plate:
  22. Attention: Secure the ensemble against falling before loosening the screws.
    Loosen the screws that connect the cylinder to the mounting plate.
  23. Adjust the position of the cylinder and hold it in place.
  24. Tighten the screws again.
  25. If necessary, adjust the position of the gripper:
  26. Attention: Secure the ensemble against falling before loosening the screws.
    Loosen the screws that connect the RC servo to the gripper.
  27. Adjust the position and orientation of the gripper and hold it in place.
  28. Tighten the screws again.
  29. Loosen the screws that connect the jaws to the gripper.
  30. Adjust the position of the jaws and hold them in place.
  31. Tighten the screws again.
  32. Mount the sensors:Mount the sensors:
  33. Slide the pneumatic proximity switches into the corresponding groove of the cylinder and fix them with the screw.
  34. Mount the limit switch in the corresponding recess.
  35. Slowly pull back the carriage to test whether it activates the limit switch.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: You can hear an audible click, when the switch is activated.
  36. If necessary, adjust the position of the limit switch and test again.
ResultThe gantry robot is fully assembled.
AFTER COMPLETING THE TASK
  • Connect the stepper motor.
  • Connect the RC servo.
  • Connect the piston rod cylinder.
  • Connect the gripper.
