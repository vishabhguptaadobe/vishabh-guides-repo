Inspecting the electrical equipment

All electrical equipment must be regularly inspected, at least once every month.
  • Connect loose wires again and replace broken wires.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Missing connections can cause the robot to malfunction. Loose wires can also cause short circuits and pose a fire hazard.
  • Replace wires with broken insulation.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Wires with broken insulation can cause the robot to malfunction. Wires with broken insulation pose a fire hazard.
  • Secure all wires that are not properly secured.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Wires that are not properly secured can be damaged by moving parts of the robot. All wires must be secured with zip ties or routed through cable ducts. All moving wires must be routed through the cable chain.
  • Replace damaged components.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Damaged components may cause the robot to malfunction.
