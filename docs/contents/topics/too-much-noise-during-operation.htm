Too much noise during operation

Too much noise during operation

High noise emission from pneumatic equipment is a health risk, wastes energy, and indicates the risk of damage to equipment.

Leaking connections

  1. Check whether all tubes are connected properly and fix improper connections.
  2. Check for leaking tubes and replace them.
  3. Check for leaking fittings and repair or replace them.
  4. Check for leaking valves and repair or replace them.

High actuator speed

When running at high speeds, actuators can hit the end-positions.
  1. Adjust the end-position cushioning.
  2. Lower the speed of the actuator.

Broken silencers

  1. Check audibly for broken silencers.
  2. Replace broken silencers.
