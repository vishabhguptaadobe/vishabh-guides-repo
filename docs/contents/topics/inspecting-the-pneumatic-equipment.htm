Inspecting the pneumatic equipment

All pneumatic equipment must be regularly inspected, at least once every month.
PREREQUISITE
Warning:
  • Automatic start-up
  • Parts can move unexpectedly when working on an operational machine, causing serious and potentially deadly injuries.
  • If possible, disconnect or switch off parts of the machine that do not need to be operational.
  • Secure the machine against unintended start-up.
  • Lower the speed of pneumatic actuators drastically before conducting inspection work.
  • Switch on the compressed air supply and the electrical power supply before replacing any parts.
  • Switch off the compressed air supply and the electrical power supply before replacing any parts.
  • Inspect the sub-circuit controlling the piston rod cylinder.
    1. Press and hold button S1 to advance the cylinder.
    2. Observe the behavior of all parts of the sub-circuit.
      ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Pay attention to leaky connections, malfunctioning components, and unusual noises.
    3. Release button S1 to retract the cylinder.
    4. Observe the behavior of all parts of the sub-circuit.
      ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Pay attention to leaky connections, malfunctioning components, and unusual noises.
  • Inspect the sub-circuit controlling the gripper.
    1. Press and hold button S2 to close the gripper.
    2. Observe the behavior of all parts of the sub-circuit.
      ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Pay attention to leaky connections, malfunctioning components, and unusual noises.
    3. Release button S2 to open the gripper.
    4. Observe the behavior of all parts of the sub-circuit.
      ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Pay attention to leaky connections, malfunctioning components, and unusual noises.
  • Connect loose tubes again and replace broken tubes.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Missing connections can cause the robot to malfunction. Leaky or improper connections produce noise and waste energy. Loose tubes can also lash out and cause injury or damage to equipment.
  • Secure all tubes that are not properly secured.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Tubes that are not properly secured can be damaged by moving parts of the robot. All tubes must be secured with zip ties or routed through cable ducts. All moving tubes must be routed through the cable chain.
  • Replace damaged components.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Damaged components may cause the robot to malfunction.
  • Replace leaky components.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Leaky components produce noise and waste energy. Leakage can be heard and felt.
