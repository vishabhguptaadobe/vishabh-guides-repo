Operating principle

The robot moves items along a horizontal line and can change their orientation.
Attention:
  • Damage to equipment
  • Over time, the pressure in the cylinder and the gripper decreases. Therefore, the gripper may open or the piston may slowly extend, causing damage to equipment.
  • Always set down a picked-up item before switching off the robot.
  • Always set down a picked-up item before leaving the robot for an extended amount.
  • Ensure sufficient clearance for the piston to safely advance.

The standard operating principle of the gantry robot is as follows:
  1. The stepper motor drives the carriage.
  2. Servo turns the gripper to target orientation.
  3. Piston rod cylinder advances to move the gripper to the target position.
  4. Gripper closes and grabs the item.
  5. Piston retracts to lift up the item.
  6. Stepper motor turns drives the carriage to the target position.
  7. Servo turns the item to the new target orientation.
  8. Piston advances to lower the item to the new position.
  9. Gripper opens and releases the item.
  10. Piston retracts to lift up the gripper.
  11. Repeat the procedure for the next item.
