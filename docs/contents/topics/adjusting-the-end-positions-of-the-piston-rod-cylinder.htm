Adjusting the end positions of the piston rod cylinder

The Pneumatic proximity sensorSensor that senses the proximity of a magnet mounted on the piston of piston rod cylinder using the Hall effect. mounted on the Piston rod cylinderPneumatic actuator consisting of a piston moving linearly in a sealed barrel. must be adjusted to correctly sense the end positions.

PREREQUISITE
Warning:
  • Automatic start-up
  • Parts can move unexpectedly when working on an operational machine, causing serious and potentially deadly injuries.
  • If possible, disconnect or switch off parts of the machine that do not need to be operational.
  • Secure the machine against unintended start-up.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is fully installed.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is switched off via directional control valve 0Z2.
  • The electric control circuit is fully installed.
  • The electric control circuit is switched off.
  • If the speed of the piston rod cylinder is not already adjusted, set the cylinder to a very low speed.
The piston rod of the cylinder is fitted with a magnet at the rear end. Two reed sensors are mounted on the cylinder that sense the proximity of this magnet. The sensors output a signal once the piston rod reaches the corresponding end position. The output of these proximity sensors can be used by Programmable logic controllerRugged computer specifically designed for automating industrial processes. Most PLCs are designed according to IEC 61131..
  1. Loosen the screw that fixes sensor 1B2.
  2. Pull out the piston rod manually as far as possible.
  3. Slide sensor 1B2 back and forth until the visual indicator on the sensor indicates activation.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: You can also hear a click when the sensor is activated.
  4. Tighten the screw that fixes sensor 1B2.
  5. Loosen the screw that fixes sensor 1B1.
  6. Push in the piston rod manually as far as possible.
  7. Slide sensor 1B1 back and forth until the visual indicator on the sensor indicates activation.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: You can also hear a click when the sensor is activated.
  8. Tighten the screw that fixes sensor 1B1.
  9. Switch on the pneumatic load circuit via valve 0Z2.
  10. Press and hold button S1 to extend the piston rod.
    STEP RESULT: 1B2 is activated once the piston is fully extended.
  11. Release button S1 to retract the piston rod.
    STEP RESULT: 1B1 is activated once the piston is fully retracted.
ResultThe end positions are set. The proximity sensors will output a signal once the piston reaches the corresponding end position.
