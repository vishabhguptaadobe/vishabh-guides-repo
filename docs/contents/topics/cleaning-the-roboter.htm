Cleaning the robot

Cleaning the robot regularly prevents faults. You can combine cleaning the robot with a partial inspection of the robot.
PREREQUISITE
Caution:
  • Hot surface
  • Electrical actuators heat up during operation. Touching hot surfaces can cause injuries.
  • Let the actuators cool down before touching them.
Tools needed:
  • Cloth
  • Brush
Supplies needed:
  • Ethanol
  • Lubrication oil
  • Remove residual materials from the gripper jaws.
  • Remove residual materials between the RC servo and the gripper.
  • Pull out and clean the piston rod.
  • Clean the leadscrew with a brush and add some drops of oil.
  • Wipe all surfaces with a cloth and ethanol.
