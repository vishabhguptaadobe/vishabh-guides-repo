Connecting the RC servo

The connections of the RC servoRC servos are small-scale electric motors with integrated position or speed control. The position of an RC servo is usually controlled via pulse-width modulation. must be connected to the terminal rail to enable controlling the motor.
PREREQUISITE
  • The gantry robot is assembled.
  • The terminal rail is mounted in a proper location.
Tools needed:
  • Cable cutter
  • Wire stripper
  • Cable stripper
  • Crimping tool
  • Slotted screwdriver
Supply needed:
  • Cable with three 0.5-mm2 wires in red, black, and brown
  • Wire-end ferrules
  • Zip ties
  1. Cut the cable to the appropriate length (> 4 m).
  2. Strip the cable at both ends.
  3. Strip and crimp the wires at both ends.
  4. Connect RC servo output V+ to the red wire.
  5. Connect RC servo output GND to the black wire.
  6. Connect RC servo output CRTL to the brown wire.
  7. Pull the cable through the cable chain.
  8. Connect the red wire to connector 13 of relay K3.
  9. Connect the black wire to 0 V.
  10. Connect the brown wire to terminal 13.
  11. Secure the cable with zip ties.
