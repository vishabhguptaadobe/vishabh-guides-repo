General safety notices

These safety notices apply generally over the entire life cycle of the gantry robot.
  • The gantry robot has fast-moving parts. Getting hit by fast-moving parts may cause serious injuries or death. During maintenance and installation, the robot must be secured against automatic start-up. In operation, the gantry robot must be properly fenced off.
  • By default, the gantry robot is not equipped with emergency-stop devices, emergency-off devices, or emergency-power-off devices. When integrating the robot into a larger machine, the robot must be equipped with appropriate safety devices.
  • The electrical equipment of the robot is not suitable for use in explosive atmospheres. The robot must not be used in explosive atmospheres.
  • Switch off the pneumatic pressure when carrying out pneumatic work on the gantry robot.
  • Switch off the electrical power when carrying out electrical work.
