Adjusting the speed of the piston rod cylinder

The speed of the piston rod cylinder is adjusted via the Throttle-check non-return valveFunctionally integrated pneumatic component that combines a throttle-check valve with a parallel non-return valve. Throttle-check non-return valves are used to regulate airflow in a given direction..
PREREQUISITE
Warning:
  • Automatic start-up
  • Parts can move unexpectedly when working on an operational machine, causing serious and potentially deadly injuries.
  • If possible, disconnect or switch off parts of the machine that do not need to be operational.
  • Secure the machine against unintended start-up.
  • The gantry robot is fully assembled.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is fully installed.
  • The pneumatic load circuit is switched on via valve 0Z2.
  • The electric control circuit is fully installed.
  • The electric control circuit is switched on.
  • Any controllers that can cause automatic start-up are switched off or disconnected.
  • The piston of cylinder 1A is retracted.
  • If the end positions of the cylinder are not already adjusted, ensure that the piston can safely extend and retract fully. Alternatively, adjust the end positions first.
The speed is adjusted under neutral load conditions. In operation, different loads may affect the speed considerably. The appropriate speed depends on the application.
The throttle-check non-return valves throttle the exhaust air to prevent rapid pressure drops and slip-stick effects. 1V2 controls retraction speed. 1V3 controls extension speed.
  1. Turn the adjusting screws of 1V2 and 1V3 clockwise until the valves are completely closed.
  2. Turn both adjusting screws counter-clockwise by 10 ° to open the valves again a bit.
  3. Check the extension speed:
    1. Press and hold button S1 to extend the piston rod.
    2. Observe the extension speed.
  4. Check the retraction speed:
    1. Release button S1 to retract the piston rod.
    2. Observe the retraction speed.
  5. Adjust the extension speed of the piston by turning the adjusting screw of 1V3.
    • Clockwise: decrease speed.
    • Counter-clockwise: increase speed.
  6. Adjust the retraction speed of the piston by turning the adjusting screw of 1V2.
    • Clockwise: decrease speed.
    • Counter-clockwise: increase speed.
  7. Manually activate and release valve 1V1 to check whether the speed is appropriate.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: If the speed is not appropriate, continue adjusting the speed.
AFTER COMPLETING THE TASK
  • Adjust the end-position cushioning of the piston rod cylinder.
  • Adjust the speed of the gripper.
