Pneumatic actuators do not move

Pneumatic actuators do not move

Pneumatic actuators do not move when activating the corresponding relays.

No electricity

If the neither the relays nor the directional control valve respond to proper input, missing electric power may cause the trouble.
  • Verify that the electricity is switched on.
  • Verify that all parts of the electric control circuit are connected properly.

No pneumatic power

If the relays and the directional control valve respond to proper input, missing pneumatic power may cause the trouble.
  • Verify that the pneumatic pressure is switched on (valve 0Z2).
  • Verify that all parts of the pneumatic load circuit are connected properly.

Fully closed throttle-check valve

If the relays and the directional control valve respond to proper input, and pneumatic pressure is switched on, a fully-closed Throttle-check non-return valveFunctionally integrated pneumatic component that combines a throttle-check valve with a parallel non-return valve. Throttle-check non-return valves are used to regulate airflow in a given direction. may cause the trouble.
  1. Warning: Opening throttle-check valves under pressure may cause the actuator to move, potentially causing serious injuries or death.
    Switch off the pneumatic pressure via valve 0Z2.
  2. Check that throttle-check non-return valves of the actuator are not fully closed.
  3. If necessary, adjust the throttle-check non-return valves.
  4. Switch on the pneumatic pressure via valve 0Z2.
