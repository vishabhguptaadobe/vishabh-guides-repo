Electrical actuators do not move

Electrical actuators do not move

Wrong wiring

Verify that the actuator is wired correctly.

Insufficient power

Electrical actuators can stall if the available power is not sufficient to accelerate the connected load.
  • Reduce the weight of the items.
  • Lower the acceleration.
  • Check that the power supply can deliver the required amperage

Actuator blocked

Actuators can accidentally get blocked kinetically, for example in end positions.
  1. Check whether the actuator is blocked.
  2. Warning: The actuator can move rapidly once the blockage is removed, causing serious injury or death. Switch off the power supply before removing the blockage!
    If yes, remove the blockage.
