Connecting the stepper motor

The phases of the Stepper motorStepper motors are brushless DC motors where the rotation is divided into discrete steps. Stepper motors do not require a position sensor or feedback loops to move accurately to a position. and the limit switch must be connected to the terminal rail to enable controlling the motor.
PREREQUISITE
  • The gantry robot is assembled.
  • Assembling the robot
  • The terminal rail is mounted in a proper location.
  • 1
  • Electrician
  • Journeyman
  • 0:30 h
  • Wire cutter
  • Wire stripper
  • Crimping tool
  • Slotted screwdriver
  • 0.5-mm2 wires in red, green, yellow, gray, and black
  • Wire-end ferrules
  • Zip ties
Data:
  1. Note:
    • Risk of electric shock
    • Turn off the power before connecting the stepper motor.
  1. Strip and crimp the wires.
  2. Connect stepper motor output A+ to terminal 7 via the red wire.
  3. Connect stepper motor output A- to terminal 8 via the yellow wire.
  4. Connect stepper motor output B+ to terminal 9 via the gray wire.
  5. Connect stepper motor output B- to terminal 10 via the green wire.
  6. Connect the NO output of the limit switch 3B1 to terminal 11 via the black wire.
  7. Secure the wires with zip ties.
AFTER COMPLETING THE TASK
  • Turn on the power again.
